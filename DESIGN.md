## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium modern architectural manufacturing aesthetic for precision window, door, and facade mesh systems. Moving decisively away from generic local contractor templates, the interface embodies industrial engineering rigor, structural reliability, and high-end residential luxury.

The target audience spans discerning luxury homeowners, interior architects, facade engineers, and corporate infrastructure managers seeking European-grade insect and barrier shielding. The visual language conveys engineered sturdiness, transparent craftsmanship, and safety without visual obstruction.

The style fuses **Industrial Modernism** with **Architectural Minimalism**:
- Precise structural rhythm governed by subtle slate grid borders and technical metrics.
- High-contrast charcoal surfaces grounding technical authority against crisp studio-white canvases.
- High-chroma industrial safety orange accents deployed intentionally for critical interactions, status flags, and primary calls-to-action.
- Refined structural typography pairing sharp, technical grotesque headlines with exceptionally legible humanist utility copy.

## Colors

The palette balances clean structural clarity with vibrant engineered warmth:

- **Primary (`#E65100`)**: Architectural Safety Orange. Derived from high-visibility safety engineering and warm Italian hardware finishes. Used strictly for high-conversion primary actions, focal badges, key interactive highlights, and trust indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1E252B`)**: Deep Charcoal / Slate Graphite. Represents anodized aluminum profiles, powdered track finishes, and architectural framing. Serves as header bars, high-contrast dark section backdrops, and primary headline text.
- **Tertiary (`#B72E2E`)**: Precision Crimson. Reserved for crucial warranty alerts, emergency structural specifications, and safety certification callouts.
- **Neutral (`#4A5568`)**: Slate Gray. Used for balanced body prose, descriptive secondary captions, and structural grid rules.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Canvas Base**: Pure `#FFFFFF` for primary product storytelling, complemented by `#F8FAFC` for alternate architectural spec panels.
- **Dark Anchors**: `#0F1418` and `#1A2026` for persistent technical navigation bars, contrast feature slabs, and dark footer hubs.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Subtle hairline dividers using `#E2E8F0` on light backgrounds and `#334155` on dark panels. Never use heavy pure-black borders.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances engineering authority with contemporary lifestyle elegance.

- **Headline Family (Space Grotesk)**: A sharp, geometric neo-grotesque font with architectural cuts. It gives numerical engineering specs, product range titles, and hero value propositions a crisp, European manufacturing presence.
- **Body & Label Family (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly refined, clear, and balanced geometry providing optimal readability across spec sheets, architectural comparison tables, and interactive lead forms.
- **Eyebrow Tags (`label-sm`)**: Always set in uppercase with deliberate letter spacing (`0.08em`) to designate certifications, geographic coverage, or manufacturing tiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system operates on an 8pt architectural grid framework designed around modular visual balance and clear structural zones:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px max-width container with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and 3rem (`48px`) outer margins. Ensures generous negative space that honors architectural photography.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid 768px - 1024px viewport width with 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins. Multi-column specs collapse into balanced 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Fluid 360px - 480px canvas with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) margins. Interactive tiles stretch full-width while preserving precision card offsets.

Vertical layout sections rely on generous breathing room: 5rem to 7rem between major product collections to prevent information density fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural layering, crisp hairline borders, and subtle diffuse shadows rather than dramatic skeuomorphism.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas)*: Solid `#FFFFFF` or high-tech `#0F1418`.
  - *Tier 1 (Cards & Product Enclosures)*: Neutral white or elevated dark `#1A2026` bounded by 1px solid slate borders (`#E2E8F0` or `#334155`).
  - *Tier 2 (Modals, Technical Specs, Floating Controls)*: Elevated via an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 20, 24, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 20, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Architectural Framing**: Cards prioritize structural edge definition over blurry shadows. Product viewports use crisp 1px borders with an inset 1px highlight for a machined-metal feel.
- **Floating Global Actions**: Call/WhatsApp quick contact bars on mobile float pinned above content with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over an 85% translucent slate base (`rgba(15, 20, 24, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a restrained **Soft (`1`)** roundedness scale (4px base radii). 

- **Engineering Context**: Architectural extrusions, aluminum casements, and pleated mesh frames are characterized by clean, rectilinear angles. Excessively rounded or pill-shaped containers contradict the precision nature of the physical products.
- **Corner Rules**:
  - Product cards, interactive tabs, form inputs, and buttons: `0.25rem` (4px).
  - Floating badges, spec tags, and technical metrics chips: `0.125rem` (2px) or sharp.
  - Dialog windows, quote estimation cards, and image framing viewports: `0.5rem` (8px max).

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (Book Site Survey / Free Consultation)**: Rich safety orange background (`#E65100`), crisp white bold text, 4px border radius, 12px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#FF6F00` with subtle upward 1px translation.
- **Secondary Action (Technical Brochure / Direct Call)**: Deep slate container (`#1E252B`) or ghost variant with 1px slate border (`#334155`), white or slate text, seamless hover transition to orange outline.
- **Contact Channel Buttons (WhatsApp / Direct Dial)**: Dedicated split action dock utilizing industrial badge styling with high-contrast icon paired alongside monospace numerical dials.

### Product & Architectural Cards
- **Display Card**: 1px subtle slate border, edge-to-edge high-resolution photography of the installed mesh mechanism. Bottom information block features an integrated dark pill badge declaring system type (e.g., *Barrier-Free Pleated*, *Honeycomb Partition*).
- **Hover State**: Image experiences subtle 1.03x scale zoom within bounded overflow; bottom border illuminates in safety orange (`#E65100`).

### Trust Badges & Warranty Indicators
- **Warranty Emblems**: Dual-tone industrial seals (e.g., "10 Years Warranty Extruded Strips") built with deep slate backing, safety orange or gold numeric highlights, and crisp micro-labels. No low-resolution raster imagery; constructed strictly with SVG vectors and structured typography.
- **Certification Strips**: Horizontal rail containing technical partners, Italian collaboration logos, and alloy standard stamps separated by refined slate vertical dividers.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Selectors
- **Fields**: White canvas with 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 4px corner radius, 14px padding. Focus state features a sharp `#E65100` outline and a soft 2px orange glow ring (`rgba(230, 81, 0, 0.15)`).
- **Measurement Selectors**: Segmented architectural toggles for window vs. door, trackless pleated vs. roller mesh. Selected states fill with `#1E252B` with high-contrast white typography.
